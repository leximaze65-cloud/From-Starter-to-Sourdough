Project Overview

I am Alexandra, a 9th-grade student at Visalia Early Technical College. My SAE focuses on sourdough fermentation and educating others by creating lessons that teach people how to make their own sourdough.

💡

Why Sourdough?

I chose sourdough because I have always wanted to do something like this and I love to bake. I decided to combine those interests into one project.

📋

My Goals

• Learn more about sourdough fermentation and baking.
• Create clear, useful lessons for beginners.
• Share my knowledge with people who can use it.
• Educate as many people as possible.
• Help others feel capable of making sourdough themselves.
