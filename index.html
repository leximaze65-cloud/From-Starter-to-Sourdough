What is this project?

My name is Alexandra, and this website is part of my FFA SAE project. I chose sourdough because I have always wanted to do something like this and I love to bake, so I decided to put the two together. Through this project, I am learning about sourdough fermentation while creating lessons that can help other people learn to make their own sourdough.
